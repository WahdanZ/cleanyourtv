Websites as apps

A tile for a website, on the TV (its browser) and the Chromecast (its browser, BrowseHere when it's there). After saving, place them like apps in the TV and Chromecast tabs (they start under More). While one is open on the Chromecast, the phone's remote switches to the trackpad.

Scenes

Several steps with one tap, e.g. "Movie night": wake the Chromecast, VPN to the US, open Netflix. They show on the Home tab and get a phone shortcut link.

Phone shortcuts

A link per button, for your phone's lock screen, Control Center or widgets: opening it does the action, without this panel. They work at home (on the Wi-Fi). Anyone with a link can press that button, so keep them to yourself.
